# Figure 2 reconstruction contract and source audit

## Scientific contract

Core conclusion: Human interaction is relational and context-dependent. This is a quantitative grid: the top row establishes paired roles and early role discrimination; the bottom row reports turn, offline PET-stratified priority, and source-specific geometry associations. Backend: the existing Python/matplotlib workflow. Export: 183 mm width, six panels, editable PDF/SVG, 300 dpi PNG, aggregate Source Data, and source hashes. No estimator run, new test, bootstrap, CI reconstruction, or inclusion-rule change.

## Source and sample decisions

- Panel a uses all 34,850 valid case rows in the accepted round3 table, with 34,850 unique source_row and scene_unique_id and finite paired means in every row. Partners are shown in both orders for presentation; the displayed count remains 34,850 independent cases. Source Data exports the symmetric two-dimensional bin counts, not individual cases. This is the explicit RQ004-PRES-PLANE decision; 34,757 belongs to the matched-support pooled ICC in panel b.
- Panel b retains the four accepted source-specific ICCs and their case-bootstrap intervals, plus the main matched-support observed ICC and matched shuffled-partner null, and the independent implementation. The latter observed interval is unavailable and receives no fabricated bar. Source-specific counts total 34,850; primary matched-support n=34,757; independent n=34,645. These are visibly distinguished. Null intervals are the 2.5th--97.5th percentiles across 1,000 matched reshuffles, not bootstrap confidence intervals for a new fitted model. Main observed ICC uses 300 scene-cluster bootstrap samples; per-source frozen ICCs use 1,000 case bootstrap samples.
- Panel c copies all six frozen IPV and kinematics AUC estimates and intervals at 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 percent elapsed interaction. Target is the sign of agent-1 minus agent-2 mean IPV over the final quarter (progress >=0.75); positive defines final_agent1_yielder. It is not observed passing order or an externally rated yielding label. Predictions are grouped out-of-fold logistic predictions with up to five scene-group folds; confidence intervals use 300 scene-cluster bootstrap draws. Sample counts vary by window: 24,872, 26,221, 28,205, 29,677, 30,932, 31,831. Combined IPV+kinematics is retained in Source Data but not plotted, as the plan requests the two baselines.
- Panel d copies paired turn-minus-straight means and their 95% Student-t intervals (not bootstrap). Overall n=3,565, then AV2=434, Lyft=625, Waymo=1,995, nuPlan=511. The nuPlan interval includes zero and is grey. Only the accepted overall-turn group is plotted; priority-left-turn reversal is not used.
- Panel e uses the existing frozen priority_gap_pet_bins.csv ALL rows, with original case-bootstrap 95% intervals (1,000 samples, seed 42). This preserves existing intervals rather than invoking the old make_fig2.py bootstrap with a different seed. PET bins and paired-case counts: <=1 s (9,799), >1 to <=2 s (22,833), >2 s (4,863). PET is an offline descriptive stratum, not an online input or warning.
- Panel f uses only primary_contrast/dataset rows for MP_minus_nonMP and SS_minus_nonSS, eight estimates with original two-group nonparametric bootstrap intervals (1,200 samples). Duplicate contrast names in stability rows with missing effects are excluded. Each source remains separate; there is no pooled universal geometry claim.

## Suggested caption

**Figure 2 | Human interaction is relational and context-dependent.** **a**, Joint distribution of the two event-mean IPV readings in 34,850 valid interactions. Each case is displayed in both partner orders; the number of independent cases is unchanged. Colour denotes the fraction of symmetric display points in each two-dimensional bin. Dashed lines mark zero. **b**, Exchangeable intraclass correlations (ICCs) for the four sources and the pooled matched-support sample, alongside a scenario-matched reshuffling null and an independent implementation. Source-specific estimates use 34,850 cases in total; pooled matched-support n=34,757 and independent n=34,645. The independent observed ICC has no supplied interval. Error bars are the frozen 95% intervals: case bootstrap for source-specific ICCs, scene-cluster bootstrap for the pooled ICC, and the central 95% of the matched-reshuffling distribution for the null. **c**, Grouped out-of-fold AUC for discriminating the role defined by the sign of the paired mean-IPV difference in the final quarter of an interaction, using early IPV or early kinematics. Shading shows frozen scene-cluster bootstrap 95% confidence intervals; n increases from 24,872 to 31,831 cases across windows. This target does not represent measured passage order. **d**, Paired turn-minus-straight event-mean IPV differences, overall and by source, with frozen 95% Student-t intervals. The nuPlan interval includes zero. **e**, Priority-minus-nonpriority mean IPV differences and original case-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals within three offline PET strata (n=9,799, 22,833 and 4,863 paired cases). **f**, Source-specific differences for merge/pass geometry (MP minus non-MP) and straight--straight pairs (S--S minus non-S--S), with original two-group bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. All effects in d--f are in radians and remain descriptive associations. Source Data accompany the figure.
