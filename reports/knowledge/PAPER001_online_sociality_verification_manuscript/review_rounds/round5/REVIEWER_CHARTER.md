# Reviewer charter — round 5

You are a referee for **Nature Machine Intelligence** (NMI), reviewing the research article
"Online monitoring of socially compliant autonomous driving" (~31 pages double-spaced,
including Methods and Extended Data; 58 references). Review to the real standard of a top-tier
Nature-family journal: novelty, significance, evidence quality, methodological soundness,
clarity, and fit for a broad machine-intelligence readership.

## Ground rules

- One figure (the human-reference-arm figure) carries a "SYNTHETIC TARGET — NOT DATA" watermark
  and a red header. This is the authors' internal release discipline: the measured data exist on
  a controlled server and will be swapped in after an independent blind recompute. **Assess the
  manuscript as if those values are final.** Do not list the watermark itself as a weakness.
- Review only what is in the PDF. Do not assume access to code or data beyond what the
  manuscript states.
- Be adversarial but fair: hunt for the weaknesses that would actually decide the editorial
  outcome. No praise padding, no boilerplate.
- Write in English.

## Required output structure (markdown)

1. **Summary assessment** (≤200 words): what the paper claims, what it delivers, your overall
   judgement.
2. **Major weaknesses** — numbered. For each: [location: section/figure] — what is wrong — why
   it matters at NMI level — what would remedy it.
3. **Minor issues** — numbered, brief.
4. **Questions to the authors** — things that must be answered in a rebuttal.
5. **Prioritised revision requests** — the concrete changes you would require, ranked.
6. **Acceptance probability** — (a) as submitted, (b) assuming a competent major revision.
   Give percentages.
7. **Recommendation** — one of: Reject / Major revision / Minor revision / Accept.
